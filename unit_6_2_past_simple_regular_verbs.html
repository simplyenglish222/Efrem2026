A letter that changed a Saturday

Read the longer text. The highlighted verbs are regular past simple forms.

A short story

Last autumn, Marta moved house from a busy city centre to a small town near the coast. During her first week there, she looked for a part-time job and visited the local library. She enjoyed the quiet streets, but she did not know many people, so some evenings seemed rather long.

One Friday, Marta received an email from the town museum. The museum invited local residents to enter a competition. They needed short stories about “an ordinary day with an unusual ending”. Marta liked writing, so she decided to take part. She prepared a meal, made tea and worked at her kitchen table for two hours.

Her story started with a woman who waited for a long time at a bus stop. In the end, the woman noticed an old postcard on the ground. She posted a letter to the address on the card, and a few days later she received a reply from a relative she had never met. Marta finished the story late that night and returned to it the next morning. She corrected a few sentences, then emailed it to the museum.

Three weeks later, the museum called. Marta did not win first prize, but she received a special prize for the most original ending. The prize included tickets to a local theatre and a free writing course. At the course, Marta met other people who enjoyed stories as much as she did. Her move to the small town changed her life more than she expected.
